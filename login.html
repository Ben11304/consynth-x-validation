ConSynth-X

Human Evaluation of Visual Fidelity in Synthetic Construction Site Images

IRB-approved research study

Before You Begin

You will review construction site images and rate how visually realistic they appear.

Estimated duration
10–15 minutes
IRB approval
Approved by Kent State University IRB (IRB #2444)
Questions

  • You are 18 years of age or older.

How it works

  1. Enter the study — choose a unique participant name below. No password is needed.
  2. Rate images — score each construction photo from 1 (obvious fake) to 5 (indistinguishable from real). Keyboard 1–5 then Enter.

Full instructions on the Tutorial page after login.

Each evaluator must use a new, unique name. Usernames cannot be reused.
