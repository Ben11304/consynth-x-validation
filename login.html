How it works

  1. Register — pick a unique username below. No password needed.
  2. Rate images — score each construction photo from 1 (obvious fake) to 5 (indistinguishable from real). Keyboard 1–5 then Enter.

≈10–20 minutes · Your ratings contribute to ConSynth-X (Paper 1, Nature Scientific Data). Full instructions on the Tutorial page after login.

Each evaluator must use a new, unique name. Usernames cannot be reused.
