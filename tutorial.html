How to Evaluate

1

Turing Test

You will see construction site images one at a time. Decide whether each image is a real photograph or AI-generated (synthetic). Use keyboard shortcuts: R = Real, S = Synthetic.

2

Realism Rating (MOS)

Rate each synthetic image on a scale of 1 (very unrealistic) to 5 (indistinguishable from real). Focus on: artifact presence, lighting consistency, object coherence, and overall plausibility. Use keyboard 1-5 then Enter.

3

Condition Recognition

Identify the weather or lighting condition shown in each synthetic image. Choose from: Clear, Rain, Snow, Night, Night+Rain, Night+Snow, Fog, or Small/Far objects.

!

Important Notes

Take your time with each image. There is no time limit. Your response time is recorded for analysis but does not affect the study. Try to be consistent in your ratings. If unsure, go with your first impression.
